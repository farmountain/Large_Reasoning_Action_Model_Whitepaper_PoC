Reflexion-Enhanced Outer Loop
==============================

  [trajectory tau_t]
         |
         v
  [tier-3 validator v_t]
         |
    v_t = 1 ---------> accept candidate, update world model
         |
    v_t = 0
         |
         v
  [Reflect(tau_t, y_t, v_t)] --> textual critique r_t
         |
         v
  [episodic memory M_{t+1}] = Decay(M_t, rho) U {r_t}
         |
         v
  [prior update] --> Prajna re-weights candidate distribution
         |
         v
  [teacher weight update] --> teacher ensemble weights w_k adapted
         |
         v
  [next trajectory tau_{t+1}]

Convergence: loop terminates when rejection rate < epsilon
             for N_plateau consecutive epochs.
